NextStep

Support for returning home, one step at a time.

Coming home brings a lot to think about. NextStep helps you sort through it, one area at a time.

Where would you like to begin?

Everyone's situation is different. Start with whatever feels most pressing to you.

  • Vital Documents

    Getting copies of important papers, like a state ID, birth certificate, or Social Security card.

  • Transportation

    Ways to get around, like public transit or a driver's license, so you can reach work, appointments, and family.

  • Housing

    Finding a safe place to stay, whether for tonight or for the longer term.

  • Employment

    Looking for work, building a resume, and preparing for interviews.

  • Benefits

    Learning about programs that may help with food, health care, or income.

NextStep shares general information only. It is not legal advice.
